How it works

Step one

The conversation

Before any camera comes out, we talk — how the day actually feels to you, who matters in the frames, what you’re afraid of missing. Shot lists are built from that, not from a template.

Step two

Chasing the light

We scout two locations against the sun, not the calendar. Golden hour and candlelight are the plan; everything else is a graceful fallback. Mid-day shoot under a flat sky is the one thing we never book.

Step three

The session itself

Unhurried. Direction only when it helps — most of what we deliver is the moment between the instructions. If someone’s laughing at something nobody else heard, that’s the frame.

Step four

Editing & delivery

Culled down to what you’d actually print, colour-matched to the day’s warmth, delivered in a private gallery within ten days. Archives are kept for five years — reprinting an old session is always possible.
